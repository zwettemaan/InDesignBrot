## Privacy Policy Draft

InDesignBrot does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

The plugin does not contact remote servers or third-party services.

InDesignBrot does use local-only loopback communication to `localhost.tgrg.net` so the UXP panel can communicate with PluginInstaller on the same machine.

No analytics, tracking, advertising, or remote telemetry are included.
